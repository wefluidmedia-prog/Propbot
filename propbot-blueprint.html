📱 Phone Number System
🔑
Current System: New Number
You pre-buy Vobiz phone numbers and seed them into the pool via /admin. Each agent who signs up gets one of these numbers automatically. They do NOT give you their existing number — they get a fresh PropBot number.
🔄
Alternative: Call Forwarding
Not built yet. This would let agents keep their existing number and set up call forwarding to the PropBot number. Simpler for agents, but requires them to configure forwarding on their own phone — can be confusing.
📞
Why New Number Is Better for MVP
Agents won't trust handing over their personal number. A dedicated business number is a feature, not a downside — "Put this on your listings, AI answers 24/7." Less risk, cleaner separation.

🎯 Your Sales Workflow (First 10 Customers)

Manual White-Glove Onboarding — Do This Until Bugs Are Fixed

1
Call the Real Estate Agent
"I have an AI receptionist that answers your property calls 24/7 in Hindi and English. It captures caller details — name, budget, area — and emails you instantly. Want to try it free for 14 days?"
2
Sign Them Up Yourself
Go to propbot.co.in/signup → enter their business details → pick a voice → paste their listings (ask them to WhatsApp you their listings). System auto-creates their AI agent + assigns a phone number.
3
Test Call Their Number
Call the PropBot number yourself. Pretend to be a buyer: "I'm looking for a 2BHK in Andheri, budget around 80 lakhs." Confirm the AI responds correctly and the lead shows up in dashboard.
4
Hand Over the Number
"Here's your AI receptionist number: +91 XXXXXXXXXX. Put this on your property listings, your JustDial page, and your website. Every call gets answered and you'll get an email with the lead details."
5
Follow Up in 3 Days
Check if they got any leads. Show them their dashboard. If happy, ask them to subscribe. If issues, fix their knowledge base or adjust the AI greeting.
