## Part 1 — Signup Walkthrough (do this first, ~10 min)

I cannot create the account for you — Fiverr requires phone + email + (sometimes) ID verification that only you can complete on your device. Follow these steps; every field you'll be asked is pre-written in Part 2 below.

1. Go to **https://www.fiverr.com/join**
2. Sign up with your Gmail (`percystatham@gmail.com`) — using Google SSO is fastest.
3. **Choose a username.** Fiverr usernames are permanent and are your public URL. Pick one now:
   - Good: `percy_builds`, `percy_ai`, `percystatham`, `voiceagent_dev`, `propbot_builder`
   - Avoid numbers and underscores where possible; keep it 8–14 chars.
4. Verify email (click the link Fiverr sends) → verify phone (SMS code).
5. Fiverr will drop you into the dashboard. Switch from "Buyer" to **"Become a Seller"** (top right menu).
6. Complete the seller onboarding wizard using **Part 2** below for every field.
7. Create **Gig #1** using Part 3 content. Save as draft if you need to come back.
8. Add Gig #2 and #3 (Parts 4 and 5) over the next day — you only need 1 live gig to start receiving orders.

> **Tip:** Fiverr lets you skip the 1-min intro video. Skip it on day 1; record and add it in week 2 once you've seen how other sellers in your category present themselves.

## Part 8 — Things You Will Be Tempted To Do, Don't

1. **Don't** buy fake reviews. Fiverr detects them and will ban permanently — I've seen it happen.
2. **Don't** pad the description with generic "I am a passionate developer" fluff. Every sentence should be a fact about what you'll deliver.
3. **Don't** price the Basic tier below $50 in AI Agents — low prices attract the worst buyers on Fiverr.
4. **Don't** promise same-day delivery. Your cushion is what lets you over-deliver, and over-delivery is what gets you 5-star reviews.
5. **Don't** forget to turn on 2FA on the Fiverr account — withdrawal fraud is real.
