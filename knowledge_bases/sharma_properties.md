# Frequently Asked Questions

## Q1: Home loan ke liye kya process hai? / What is the home loan process?
Most banks offer 80% financing on property value. We have tie-ups with SBI, HDFC Bank, and ICICI Bank for pre-approved loans. Typical interest rate: 8.5-9.5% per annum. Processing time: 7-10 working days. Documents needed: PAN card, Aadhar, salary slips (3 months), bank statements (6 months), ITR (2 years). We help you with the entire process free of charge.

## Q2: Delhi mein registration charges kitne hain? / What are registration charges in Delhi?
Stamp duty in Delhi: 6% for men, 4% for women. Registration fee: 1% of property value. So total additional cost is approximately 5-7% on top of property price. For example, on an ₹85 lakh flat, registration + stamp duty for a woman buyer would be around ₹4.25 lakh.
