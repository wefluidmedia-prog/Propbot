## Outreach Rules

1. **Timing:** Send messages 10 AM - 1 PM or 4 PM - 7 PM (agent free time)
2. **Personalize:** Always mention their area/listing ("Dwarka mein aapke listings dekhe")
3. **Volume:** Max 10 new WhatsApp contacts/day per number (avoid spam flag)
4. **Wait 10-15 min between messages** to different contacts
5. **Use 2 WhatsApp numbers** (personal + business) to double capacity
6. **Voice notes > text** for follow-ups (higher reply rate)
7. **Reply within 15 min** to any response — speed wins deals
8. **Never send same message twice** — always personalize
