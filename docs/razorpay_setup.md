# Razorpay Personal Account — Payment Link Setup (No GST)

## Step 1: Create Account
1. Go to https://dashboard.razorpay.com/signup
2. Sign up with your personal email + phone
3. Select "Individual / Freelancer" as business type
4. Enter PAN card number (personal PAN works)
5. No GST number required — leave blank

## Step 2: Complete KYC
1. Upload PAN card photo
2. Upload Aadhar card (front + back)
3. Bank account details (your personal savings account is fine)
4. Razorpay approval takes 1-3 business days

## Step 3: Create Payment Link (per client)
1. Dashboard → Payment Links → Create New
2. Fill in:
   - **Amount**: ₹5,000
   - **Description**: "Sharma Properties — AI Receptionist Monthly Subscription"
   - **Accept partial payments**: No
   - **Expiry**: Leave blank (no expiry for recurring manual billing)
3. Click Create
4. Copy the link → share with client via WhatsApp/SMS

## Step 4: Monthly Billing Workflow
1. Each month, send the same payment link to the client
2. Client pays via UPI / debit card / credit card / netbanking
3. You get notified in Razorpay dashboard + email
4. Money settles in your bank account within 2 business days

## Fees
- UPI: 2% + 18% GST = ~2.36% (₹118 on ₹5,000)
- Debit card: 2% + 18% GST = ~2.36%
- Credit card: 2% + 18% GST = ~2.36%
- **Net revenue per client**: ~₹4,882/month

## Limitations (Personal Account)
- ₹40 lakh annual turnover limit before GST registration required
- At 3 clients × ₹5,000/month = ₹1.8L/year — well under limit
- Register GST when approaching 8-10 clients

## When to Register GST
- Annual turnover > ₹20 lakh (service provider threshold)
- Or when you want to deduct input tax credit on expenses
- Switch to Razorpay business account after registration
