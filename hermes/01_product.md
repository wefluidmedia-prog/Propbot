# PropBot — Product Overview

## What It Is
PropBot is an AI-powered receptionist built specifically for Indian real estate agents. It answers phone calls 24/7 in Hindi, English, and Hinglish — qualifying buyers, capturing leads, and booking site visits automatically.

**Website:** https://propbot.co.in

## The Problem We Solve
Indian real estate agents lose deals because they miss calls — especially at night, during site visits, or on weekends. Hiring a human receptionist costs Rs 20,000+/month and they only work 8 hours. Leads call once and move on if nobody picks up.

**One missed call at 2 AM could be a Rs 50 lakh deal.**

## How It Works
1. Agent signs up on propbot.co.in (60 seconds)
2. Adds their property listings
3. Chooses an AI voice (6 natural Indian voices — male & female)
4. Gets a dedicated phone number
5. AI handles all incoming calls — qualifies leads, captures details, books site visits
6. Agent sees everything in a real-time dashboard

## Key Features
- **24/7 AI Voice Receptionist** — answers every call, never sleeps
- **Hindi + Hinglish + English** — speaks like a real Indian receptionist
- **Lead Capture** — name, phone, budget, area preference, property type, urgency
- **Site Visit Booking** — schedules viewings automatically
- **Real-Time Dashboard** — leads, call history, recordings, transcripts
- **Email Alerts** — instant notification for every new lead
- **Chat Widget** — embeddable on agent's website for text-based leads
- **Self-Serve** — no onboarding calls, no technical skills needed

## What Makes PropBot Different
- **India-first**: Built for Indian real estate, not adapted from a Western product
- **Language**: Fluent Hindi/Hinglish, not translated English
- **Price**: Rs 2,499-4,999/month vs Rs 20,000+ for a human
- **Niche focus**: Real estate specific — understands property queries, budgets, site visits
- **Instant setup**: Live in 5 minutes, not days

## Tech Stack (for reference, not for posting)
- Voice AI: Bolna.ai + Vobiz.ai (Indian telephony)
- Backend: FastAPI + Supabase (PostgreSQL)
- AI: Claude Sonnet 4
- Hosting: Render (always-on)
- Billing: Razorpay
