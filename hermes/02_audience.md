# PropBot — Target Audience & ICP

## Primary ICP: Indian Real Estate Agents/Brokers

### Who They Are
- Solo brokers or small teams (1-5 people)
- Operating in Indian metros: Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bangalore, Pune, Hyderabad, Chennai
- Age: 28-55, male-dominated industry
- Tech comfort: use WhatsApp and smartphones daily, but not "tech-savvy"
- Revenue: Rs 5-50 lakh/month in commissions
- Language: Hindi primary, English secondary, heavy code-switching (Hinglish)

### Their Pain Points
1. **Missed calls = missed deals** — they're on site visits, in meetings, sleeping
2. **Can't afford a full-time receptionist** — Rs 20,000+/month plus management overhead
3. **No CRM** — leads tracked in WhatsApp chats and notebooks
4. **Night/weekend leads lost** — property searches happen at 10 PM on Sundays
5. **Repetitive questions drain time** — "kya price hai?", "kitna sqft?", "site visit kab?"

### What They Care About
- **Saving money** (cost vs human receptionist is the #1 hook)
- **Not missing leads** (fear of lost revenue)
- **Looking professional** to clients
- **Simplicity** — they won't read docs or attend onboarding calls

### Where They Hang Out Online
- **Twitter/X**: Indian real estate community, proptech discussions
- **Instagram**: Property showcase reels
- **WhatsApp**: Business groups, broker networks
- **YouTube**: Property walkthrough videos
- **LinkedIn**: Professional networking (less active)
- **Facebook groups**: Local broker communities ("Delhi NCR Brokers")

### How They Make Buying Decisions
- Word of mouth from other brokers
- WhatsApp forwards ("bhai ye dekh, mast tool hai")
- Free trial — they won't pay without trying
- ROI proof — "ye Rs 5,000/month bachaega Rs 20,000"
- Trust in the founder/brand (personal connection matters in India)

## Secondary Audience (Future — NOT now)
- Property developers wanting lead qualification
- Real estate agencies (multi-agent teams)
- Other service businesses needing AI receptionists (dentists, salons, etc.)
