# PropBot — Twitter Marketing Strategy

## Goal
Build audience + generate inbound leads through Twitter/X. Establish PropBot as the go-to AI receptionist for Indian real estate.

## Content Pillars (Rotate Daily)

### 1. Problem Awareness (Mon, Thu)
Highlight the pain of missed calls and lost deals.
- "How many calls did you miss this week?"
- Real scenarios: 2 AM calls, calls during site visits
- Statistics about lead response time and conversion

### 2. Product Showcase (Tue, Fri)
Show what PropBot actually does — screenshots, demos, results.
- Dashboard screenshots
- Sample AI conversations (Hindi/Hinglish)
- Feature highlights
- Before/after comparisons

### 3. Build in Public (Wed)
Share the founder journey — wins, failures, learnings.
- Milestones: "shipped X today"
- Challenges: "building voice AI in Hindi is hard because..."
- Behind the scenes of a solo founder

### 4. Industry Insights (Sat)
Real estate trends, tips for agents.
- "5 ways brokers lose deals without knowing"
- Market trends in Indian real estate
- Technology adoption in Indian real estate

### 5. Social Proof / Engagement (Sun)
Testimonials, case studies, community engagement.
- Customer results (when available)
- Polls: "Would you trust an AI to answer your business calls?"
- Engagement threads

## Posting Cadence
- 1-2 tweets per day
- 1 thread per week (deep dive on a topic)
- Reply to relevant conversations in real estate / AI / startup spaces
- Engage with Indian founder community

## Target Accounts to Engage With
- Indian real estate influencers
- PropTech founders
- AI/SaaS builders in India
- #BuildInPublic community
- Startup India ecosystem

## CTA Strategy
- Not every tweet needs a CTA
- When including CTA: "Try free for 14 days at propbot.co.in"
- Soft CTAs: "DM me if you're a real estate agent losing calls"
- Link in bio always points to propbot.co.in

## Metrics to Track
- Follower growth
- Engagement rate (replies, retweets, likes)
- Link clicks to propbot.co.in
- DMs from potential customers
- Signups attributed to Twitter
