# PropBot — Voice, Tone & Content Guidelines

## Brand Voice
- **Confident but not arrogant** — we know our product works
- **Direct and simple** — no jargon, no fluff
- **Indian context first** — use Rs not $, reference Indian cities, Hindi phrases OK
- **Builder mindset** — solo founder building in public, relatable underdog energy
- **Practical, not hype** — show real value, not "revolutionary AI disruption"

## Tone on Twitter
- Casual, conversational — like texting a smart friend
- Mix of English and Hinglish is OK (reflects the audience)
- Short, punchy tweets — no essays
- Use numbers and specifics ("saves Rs 15,000/month" not "saves money")
- Stories > features ("A broker in Noida missed a Rs 60L deal at 2 AM. Here's how to never let that happen.")

## Content Themes
1. **Problem awareness** — "How many calls did you miss this week?"
2. **Cost comparison** — receptionist vs PropBot math
3. **Build in public** — sharing the journey, milestones, learnings
4. **Real estate industry insights** — trends, tips for agents
5. **Product updates** — new features, improvements
6. **Social proof** — testimonials, case studies (when available)
7. **Founder journey** — solo founder, bootstrapped, India-focused

## What NOT to Post
- Don't trash competitors directly
- Don't overpromise ("replace your entire team with AI")
- Don't use Western-centric examples — keep it Indian
- Don't be salesy in every tweet — 80% value, 20% promotion
- Don't use corporate buzzwords ("synergy", "leveraging AI", "paradigm shift")
- Don't claim features that don't exist yet (outbound calls are "coming soon", not live)
- Don't post technical implementation details (no one cares about FastAPI or Supabase)

## Sample Tweet Styles

**Problem hook:**
"Indian real estate agents lose 3-5 leads every week just because nobody picked up the phone. At 2 AM, on weekends, during site visits. That's Rs 50L+ in lost deals per year."

**Cost comparison:**
"Human receptionist: Rs 20,000/month, works 8 hours
PropBot AI: Rs 2,499/month, works 24/7

Do the math."

**Build in public:**
"Shipped outbound call verification for PropBot today. Your AI can now call leads back within 60 seconds. Solo founder things."
