# PropBot — Competitive Positioning

## What Alternatives Exist

### 1. Human Receptionist
- Cost: Rs 15,000-25,000/month
- Works 8 hours, takes leave, needs training
- Can't handle multiple calls simultaneously
- No automatic lead capture or CRM
- **PropBot advantage**: 75% cheaper, 24/7, never misses a call

### 2. IVR / Automated Phone Menus
- "Press 1 for sales, press 2 for..."
- Callers hate IVR, high drop-off rates
- No lead qualification — just routing
- **PropBot advantage**: Natural conversation, actually qualifies leads

### 3. Generic AI Voice Tools (VAPI, Bland, etc.)
- Not India-focused — poor Hindi/Hinglish support
- Require technical setup, API integration
- Not built for real estate workflow
- Expensive per-minute pricing
- **PropBot advantage**: India-first, real estate-specific, flat pricing, zero-tech setup

### 4. CRM Tools (Zoho, Salesforce)
- Don't answer phones
- Complex, expensive, overkill for solo brokers
- **PropBot advantage**: Answers calls AND captures leads, much simpler

### 5. Missing Calls / Voicemail
- Most Indian callers don't leave voicemails
- They just call the next broker
- **PropBot advantage**: Answers instantly, captures the lead before they call someone else

## Our Positioning Statement
"PropBot is the first AI receptionist built specifically for Indian real estate agents — answers every call in Hindi & English 24/7, captures leads, and books site visits. From Rs 2,499/month."

## Key Differentiators
1. **India-first** — Hindi, Hinglish, Indian voices, Razorpay billing, Indian phone numbers
2. **Real estate-specific** — understands property queries, budgets, localities, site visits
3. **Self-serve** — live in 5 minutes, no onboarding calls
4. **Affordable** — flat monthly pricing, no per-minute charges
5. **Complete solution** — voice + chat + dashboard + alerts, not just an API
